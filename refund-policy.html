We make everything by hand and want you to love it. If something isn't right, here's how we put it right.

Returns

You can return accessories within 30 days of delivery for a refund or exchange. Items must be unused, unwashed and in their original condition and packaging.

To start a return, email surfboards@wawawave.com with your order number. Please don't send anything back before we've replied with the return address.

Return shipping

You pay the cost of returning an item, unless it arrived faulty or we sent the wrong item. We recommend a tracked service — we can't refund parcels that go missing on the way back to us.

Faulty or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We'll repair, replace or refund it, and cover the shipping. This doesn't affect your rights under the Consumer Protection Act.

Handmade goods vary slightly in grain, colour and finish. That's the nature of the craft, not a defect.

Refunds

Once we've received and checked your return, we'll refund the original payment method within 10 business days. Original shipping costs are not refunded, except for faulty or wrong items.

Exceptions

  • Made-to-order and custom boards are arranged by enquiry and covered by the terms we agree with you directly.
  • Make Your Own Surfboard and gift cards are non-refundable, but can be rescheduled or transferred to someone else. They are valid for 3 years from purchase.
  • Sale items can't be returned unless faulty.
