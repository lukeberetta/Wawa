This policy explains how Wawa Surfboards ("we", "us") collects and uses your personal information when you visit wawawave.com or buy from us, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Responsible party

Wawa Surfboards, 166-168 Main Rd, Muizenberg, Cape Town, 7950. Information officer: [full name], surfboards@wawawave.com.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, email, phone number, delivery and billing address, and what you bought.
  • Enquiries: what you tell us when you email or WhatsApp us about a board or a visit.
  • Payment: card payments are handled by our payment provider, Yoco, on Shopify's secure checkout. We never see or store your full card details.
  • Browsing: our shop platform, Shopify, uses cookies to run the cart and checkout and to keep the site secure.

How we use it

  • To process, ship and support your order, including returns.
  • To reply to your enquiries and arrange custom builds.
  • To meet our legal and tax record-keeping obligations.
  • To send you news or offers, only if you've opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the shop: Shopify (store and checkout), Yoco (payments) and our couriers (delivery). Some of these providers store data outside South Africa, under safeguards that protect your information to a standard comparable to POPIA.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as the law requires (generally five years), and other information only as long as we need it for the purposes above.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email surfboards@wawawave.com. If you're unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa (inforegulator.org.za).
