Honouring the source
Way out on the split ends of surfing’s fringe, exists an anachronistic little wooden surfboard company where quality, respect for our roots and a belief in the sanctity of nature are cornerstones. We build wooden surfboards and surf craft inspired by the intuited hydrodynamic genius of the ancient Hawaiian board builders.

- Short

Steve Lis-inspired Fish
With its unprecedented speed and traction, the fish was the most progressive surfboard to emerge from the 1960 shortboard revolution. Creativity fuelled by youthful energy, intuition and improvisation, the fish crew of the 1960’s surfed these aquatic ‘skateboards’ in a style that foreshadowed the coming revolution in skateboarding. A Steve Lis inspired shape accompanied by Larry Gephart inspired keel fins, our Classic Fish is one of the most versatile and enduring designs you want to pack for a surf in J’Bay, Buffels, or Bali. Available with either a domed deck or a bevelled (flat) deck option.

Gun Fish inspired by Skip Frye Larry Gephart
Steve Lis designs, the Wawa Gun Fish offers plenty drive from the centre. A tapered tail delivers speed and effortless flow in medium-sized to overhead surf. The clean taper through the tail allows quick and easy engagement of the fins. Minimal effort to unlock drive and control is required in a board suited to a wide range of conditions. This is a more performance-oriented fish, offering strong hold, tight pivot, and powerful projection. Freedom to manoeuvre, whether drawing longer lines or pushing harder off the tail, the Wawa Gun Fish responds with confidence and speed. Available with either a domed deck or a bevelled (flat) deck option.

Simmons
Inspired by the revolutionary thinking of Bob Simmons, our Mini-Simmons pays tribute to one of surfing’s original innovators. In the late 1940s, Simmons was already experimenting with fibreglass and Styrofoam and applying hydrodynamic principles to surfboard design - decades ahead of his time. His short planing hull concepts challenged convention and laid the groundwork for the modern performance board.
The Wawa Mini-Simmons carries that spirit forward.

Parallel rails, a generous outline, and a wide, powerful tail create immediate lift and effortless speed. Flat rocker and thickness through the rails honour traditional planing hull theory - flat and straight equals fast. The result is a board that accelerates quickly, carries momentum through softer sections, and feels alive underfoot. In weaker surf, it’s pure fun - playful, skatey, and exciting. When the waves step up to shoulder-high and even overhead, the board transforms into a fast, flowing dream ride, holding its line with surprising control.

Available as a performance-tuned weapon or shaped with a more classic Simmons feel for the traditionalist, this design offers endless possibilities.

Fast. Functional. Forward-thinking — just as Simmons intended.

- Long

Longboard surfers use boards 8 to 11 feet in length. Rounded noses and bottom shapes that prioritise glide, wave count, and fluid movement on smaller, mellower waves. Our boards are highly accessible for beginners, intermediate and professional surfers keen on trim, cross-stepping, and noseriding. An extended time in the surf is guaranteed with the fun that come with our longboards.

Model “T” Longboard
Inspired by Donald Takayama’s longboard shapes, the Model T is the most well-rounded and versatile design we offer. Classic and retro, the Model T features a broad, easy-to-noseride nose scoop and a wide yet surprisingly loose tail. A design for both the competitive and rider keen for some very laid back fun in the surf and sun.
Classic 50/50 rails, a single fin, square tail, it is the timber and cork over eps construction that see the Model T come alive. Timeless performance with smooth, controlled glide all yours in surf big and especially small.

Finished with a natural wood grain exterior, the board’s hydrodynamic properties enhance lift, flex, and overall feel - resulting in a uniquely smooth and connected surfing experience.

Model “L” Performance Longboard

The Model “L” is our ultra high-performance longboard - built for surfers who want speed, control, and next-level maneuverability in a classic outline. Thinner, with more rocker and less volume than our Model “T” (Takayama) and Model “P” (Pig), the Model “ “ is designed to handle steeper waves and tighter pockets with ease.

Featuring a responsive 2 + 1 fin setup, it delivers solid drive and smooth carving through turns. Super lightweight, with foiled rails, a refined nose scoop, and a fast double concave flowing into a vee tail - a quick, lively feel underfoot is all yours.

With a plan shape and foil that help reduce pearling, the Model “L” stays confident on late drops and critical sections. Fast, sleek, and highly responsive, it’s best described as a true speedster - the go-to longboard for surfers who want performance in both small and serious surf.

Model “P” Performance Longboard

Inspired by Dale Velzy’s iconic Pig model from 1955 - one of the most influential surfboard designs of the decade - the Model P marks a pivotal moment in how surfers approached wave riding. Its wide hip, set 10” back from center, delivers effortless trim and natural flow, rewarding surfers with confident wave judgment and precise timing. This is not a beginner’s board; it’s built for riders who value turns over straight lines.

Extra width through the tail allows easy pivot and control while riding deeper in the pocket, letting you harness the wave’s power to its fullest. Designed for surfers craving performance, the medium entry and exit rocker provides beautiful glide in smaller surf while staying responsive when you step back over the fin.

A classic single-fin longboard, finished with a timeless wood tail block, the Model P comes alive on dreamy, glassy days and truly shines in small to medium conditions.

- Finless

We love finless surfing. No fins, no friction, plenty speed, no problems. Our finless boards are likened to forms of sculpture that, if dialled in, will let you embrace the art of drifting at just about every Cape Point point break littered with kelp. Hop on one of our finless sculptures, head to the sea for high-line trim not comparable to anything.

Alaia Surfboard
If our Paipo boards are punk rock, then our Alaias are Miles Davis. Thin, flat rockered and sealed with oil, the Alaia is designed to be ridden primarily standup and on a rail. They glide with an unrestricted ease that modern surfboards cannot begin to compete with. Talk to anyone who has put the time into learning to ride one of these boards and you will find them waxing lyrical about the spiritual journey they have found themselves on. Unbelievably fun and entirely addictive, these boards transform days, which would otherwise be too small or weak, into adrenaline soaked, speed sessions.
Thin, flat-rockered and sealed with oil, the wooden Alaia is a surfboard unlike anything modern design has produced. Raw speed, effortless glide, and a direct connection to the wave that foam boards simply can’t replicate.

Used by ancient Hawaiians and rooted in Polynesian surf culture, the Alaia isn’t just a board — it’s a return to surfing’s origins. Unbelievably fast and ridiculously fun, the Alaia transforms small, weak days into adrenaline-soaked speed sessions. Perfect for the playful runners of Stilbaai and Jongensfontein — and once you’ve mastered the art, even J-Bay becomes a playground.

Hand-shaped from solid timber since 2010, the Wawa Alaia is a board that demands respect… but rewards you with one of the purest rides surfing has to offer. And it’s not just surfers catching on — strapless, wind-powered kiteboarders have also started embracing the Alaia, making it a fast-growing favourite for those chasing glide, flow, and freedom. Challenging at first, addictive once it clicks.

Alaia Models:

Classic Alaia
With an outline that conjures up black and white images of Waikiki, the Classic Alaia is our take on the polished wooden blade that's been sliding aquatic apes shoreward for the last couple thousand years. At 15-and-half inches wide and between 6'-6'6" in length, the Classic is the board that will make you rethink the nature of pre-European contact surfing. "Speed provides the one genuinely modern pleasure", Aldous Huxley famously said. "This board with its ancient lines disproves that and shows that speed has always been fun.

Trigger Alaia - Parabolic railed swallow tail. Inspired by the triggerfish, we go a little wider in both the nose and the tail to facilitate paddling while retaining flex. Particularly in the tail through the deep swallow. We make these as small as 5'7", but for the average adult surfer we recommend riding boards between 6'4" and 6'6".

Trick - Inspired by the boards John Wegener has been building and Rob Machado has been ripping, the Trick is a performance board. It looks a little like a snowboard. It feels a little like a snowboard and are basically a trip to ride. These boards can also be ridden really short, 5'7" and up. The Wawa Trick Alaia is hyperactive in nature!

Needle - These boards are made for bigger waves. Longer than the Classic, Trigger and Trick, the Needle design is in inspired by the needlefish. Much like its fish counterpart, the Needle is elongated and narrow. More wood means more buoyancy and easier paddling. Sizes vary from 7' to 8'.

Paipo, Boogy & Belly

Paipos, along with Alaias, were the wave riding craft of surfers in pre-European contact Hawaii. Short, wide and fast, these bodyboards became the punk rock of surfing in the twentieth century. Surfers like Valentine Chang rode them standing up in the fifties. A surfing style of the distant future. Video footage of the big wave pioneers riding giant Waimea show locals flying past the haole heroes at twice their speed on little Paipos. Our Paipos come both finned (based on boards ridden by Valentine Chang) or finless.
Paipo Models

The Bullet - Between 3'8" and 4'2", the Bullet's outline echoes the ancient Paipos. Designed to be ridden prone with the aid of swim fins, this is a fast and dynamic little bodyboard that is guaranteed to leave you grinning like a grom.

The Gogfather - A short, wide, solid wood planing hull, the Godfather's design is based on the style of board ridden by the kind of guy in the type of waves that demand respect. Built purely for speed, check out any old footage at Waimea Bay and you will see them. Guys on similar boards flat lining across the faces of monster waves as stand up surfers hobble towards the channel. 4' is the average size they come in.

Dr Strangelove - Born of a late night tryst between Jazz, sensimelia and a grinder. Dr. Strangelove is a tiny little, solid wood mini Simmons. A planing hull with twin keel fins and a pronounced kick in the nose to help making steep drops. Very steep ones. Super fun in junky little reforms and electrifying in anything jucier, Dr. Strangelove rides like a souped up skimboard with fins. Designed to be ridden standing up, we keep these boards really short. Between 3'8" and 5'9". Check out the legendary Valentine Ching on Vimeo, riding his little red paipo. The potential is endless.

Purple Haze - Using solid pieces of Himalayan timber, these boards are one off pieces. The name is attributed to the purple colour of this extra ordinary wood once sanded and oiled. Will leave you in a purple daze...

Story

Established in 2010, Wawa is the name of an African timber. Also short for 'Wood And WAves’.

The Wawa Story, as told by founder / owner / shaper Cobus

For Wawa Wooden Surfboards founder Cobus Joubert, the ocean has always been both playground and teacher. His earliest memories are of bodysurfing the waves of Plettenberg Bay – carefree days that sparked a lifelong connection to the sea. “They were the happiest place for me as a kid,” says Cobus, “and a welcome escape from working holidays involving the harvest of pears, apples and peaches on our family farm under a hot Klein Karoo sun.” That rhythm of water and wave would shape his outlook and his craft.

While studying at Stellenbosch, a sports injury nudged him from windsurfing to surfing, igniting a fascination with the surfboard as an object of design and emotion. By 2000, he shaped his first board – a challenging but formative experiment that led him away from the conventional materials of polyurethane, carcinogenic polyresin and fibreglass towards wood, a medium that felt more alive, more sustainable and more connected to surfing’s origins.

Old School, Modern Tech

Driven to blend performance with sustainability, each craft offers a hydrodynamic genius and longevity unique to timber's unique characteristics. When living nature (Paulownia timber) features and functions as intended in it’s natural form, our work is complete.

Biophillic ‘wood is good’ design
When living nature features and functions as intended in it’s natural form, our work is complete. Nature as a theme is a measure of quality in all we do.

The Sea, Our Heritage
Beyond the lure of the surf craft, is the lure of the sea, her waves of mysterious origins and destinies, her ever-flowing tides and currents that wash all islands and continents, and her undersea life. Our thoughts and actions can pollute or restore, degrade or rebuild, hurt or heal. As part of nature, we benefit from sustaining the eco-systems and surf we so depend on, and put the ocean at the heart of all decisions made in the Wawa workshop.

Product Promise
Trusted shapes, shaped by surfers, kiters and foilers. Leaning heavily on the stiff flex, torsion, good vibration and strength offered by wood combined with eps / xps all vacuumed into place, we build boards that are light, boards that perform and last.

Performance & Handling
Exceptional Manoeuvrability: Lightweight construction combined with a proven, trusted design delivers responsive handling in all conditions. Lightness: Waterproof Paulownia timber and EPS core create an ultra-light structure that’s easy to carry, and control in and out of the water. Flex Properties: Paulownia offers an outstanding stiffness-to-weight ratio - superior to fiberglass, carbon, aluminium, and steel - resulting in lively flex and controlled energy return. Float & Buoyancy: The EPS and cork combination provides excellent buoyancy, offering more float per litre of volume for improved stability and efficiency. Active Vibration Control: A minimum triple-stringer layout, timber deck and bottom, cork, and foam sandwich construction naturally dampen vibration for a smoother ride. Improved Hydro- & Aerodynamics: Over time, the Paulownia and cork exterior is subtly shaped by the ocean, creating a textured “speed skin.” Micro-bubbles collect on the surface, reducing drag, lowering friction, and optimising lift both in water (surfing) and air (foiling).

Durability & Longevity
Long-Lasting Construction: Hydrophobic timber and cork are naturally strong and resilient. Cork rails resist paddle strikes, while hardwood nose and tail blocks protect the board’s most vulnerable areas. Cool in Direct Sun: Timber and cork do not heat up like resin or fiberglass boards, keeping your board cooler and more comfortable on hot days. Easy Maintenance & Repair: The natural wood and cork exterior is simple to maintain. Minor repairs can be done with basic tools - a sharp blade, a wine cork or timber block, and glue.

Sustainable Promise
Wood is a renewable material that fosters a deeper emotional connection - encouraging care, respect, and longevity rather than disposability. Less is more: No need for fiberglass & resin (toxic and extra weight) seal, as our timber is water proof and warp resistant. Custom Shapes by Hand: Each board is crafted with the rider, surf conditions, and dream shape in mind. CNC Precision Available: CNC shaping ensures accuracy and consistency, especially for repeat orders, while preserving the integrity of the design.

Design, Identity, Emotion
Functional Art: Traditional woodworking techniques and refined finishing touches make every board durable, unique, and collectible. Emotional Resonance: More than just surf craft, our boards embody values, identity, and belonging - products you’ll be proud to ride, own, and display. Eye Candy: Natural timber patina adds rarity, individuality, and authenticity - no two boards are ever the same. A Truly Cool Product: Genuine materials, honest construction, and timeless aesthetics result in a board that stands apart in performance, feel, and visual presence.

Product Care
Rinse with salt water only: Do not rinse with fresh water. Salt water helps preserve the natural balance of the raw timber and cork. Storage: Store in a dry, well-ventilated room, away from direct sunlight. Do not store inside a board bag, as trapped moisture can affect the natural materials. Timber Protection: Occasionally apply wood wax or boiled linseed oil. Rub on, allow it to penetrate, then rub off. This nourishes and protects the raw timber surface. Repairs & Dings: Cork rail and deck dings are easy to repair. Use a blade to cut damaged area, apply wood glue, fill with a wine cork or small timber block (inlay), sand lightly and seal with wood wax or oil.
