We ship handmade goods worldwide from our workshop in Muizenberg, Cape Town. Because much of what we make is crafted by hand, please read the timing notes below.

Where we ship

We deliver across South Africa and internationally. Shipping options and costs for your address are shown at checkout.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. In-stock accessories (board covers, bags, wetsuit jackets, wax combs, beach bats, clothing and prints) ship first. The Make Your Own Surfboard experience is arranged directly with you after purchase — we'll be in touch.

Delivery time & courier

Once dispatched, expect:

  • South Africa — main centres: 2–3 business days with The Courier Guy
  • South Africa — regional & outlying areas: 3–5 business days
  • International: 3–7 business days with DHL Express

You'll receive a tracking number by email once your order is on its way.

Shipping costs

Shipping is charged by the weight of your order and shown at checkout before you pay.

  • South Africa: R120 up to 2 kg · R160 for 2–5 kg · R220 for 5–10 kg
  • International: R750 up to 2 kg · R1,100 for 2–5 kg · R1,600 for 5–10 kg
  • Collection from the workshop: free

For orders over 10 kg, email us for a quote.

Prices are in South African Rand (ZAR). Your bank converts the amount to your own currency.

Customs, duties & import taxes

International orders may be charged import duties and taxes by the destination country. These are not included in our prices or shipping costs, and are paid by the customer when the parcel arrives. Please check your country's rules before ordering.

Collection

You can choose free collection from our workshop at 166-168 Main Rd, Muizenberg at checkout. We'll let you know when your order is ready.

Surfboards & foilboards

Boards are made to order and not shipped through this store. To order a board, use the Enquire button on any board page — we'll arrange collection from the workshop or delivery, locally or overseas, by separate quote.

Address accuracy

Please double-check your delivery address at checkout. We can't be responsible for orders sent to an incorrectly entered address. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away at surfboards@wawawave.com.

Damaged in transit

If your order arrives damaged, photograph it and contact us within 7 days at surfboards@wawawave.com and we'll sort it out.
